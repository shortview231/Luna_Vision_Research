# Luna Vision Public Research Policy

This repository is the public research surface for Luna Vision.

## Publish

Material appropriate for this repository includes:

- Research summaries and literature reviews
- Citations and links to original research
- Architecture proposals suitable for public discussion
- Reproducible experiments
- Benchmark methodology and aggregate results
- Non-sensitive prototype code intended for release
- Dataset documentation when redistribution is permitted
- Failure analysis with private information removed
- Accessibility design findings

## Do Not Publish

Do not commit:

- Credentials, API keys, tokens, or secrets
- Personally identifying information
- Private household imagery or video
- Raw user recordings without explicit publication permission
- Private research journals or internal notes not reviewed for release
- Copyrighted datasets or assets without redistribution rights
- Security-sensitive internal configuration
- Claims that experimental navigation functionality is proven safe when it has not undergone appropriate validation

## Evidence Status

Public findings should identify their status whenever practical:

- Literature-supported
- Reproduced
- Experimental
- Hypothesis
- Planned

A citation to a paper is not equivalent to reproducing its result. A successful prototype demonstration is not equivalent to validating a safety-critical system.

## Reproducibility

For experimental results, record enough information to understand and reproduce the work where practical, including:

- Hardware
- Software and model versions
- Dataset or test source
- Evaluation conditions
- Metrics
- Known limitations
- Date of experiment

## Accessibility and Safety

Luna Vision research concerns technology that may eventually influence mobility decisions. Public documentation should distinguish descriptive assistance from validated navigation guidance and should document uncertainty and failure modes rather than presenting only successful examples.
